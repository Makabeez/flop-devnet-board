How this is computed

  • Sponsored: minerStaking.sponsoredStakeByMiner. Registered: minerStaking.miners. Calibrated: has a hwCalibration.benchmarkBurstCaps entry (its accepted GFLOP/s cap).
  • Quoted by gateway: listed in x-flop.price_sources of api.flop.network/v1/models.
  • Channels: every computeChannel.channels entry, by miner and by opening agent. The gateway account is the agent that opened the most channels overall.
  • Paid: realized settlements from the hosted FLOP indexer (flop_list_settlements, field minerPaid).
  • Times come from block numbers using on-chain timestamps; older dates are approximate. The snapshot is cached for 60 s.
  • Raw data: /api/snapshot (JSON, CORS open). Code: github.com/Makabeez/flop-devnet-board.
